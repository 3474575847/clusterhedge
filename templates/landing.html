The Platform

Built for the Physical Economy

Most financial tools are built for equities traders. ClusterHedge is built for people who make things — builders, fabricators, upcyclers, and small manufacturers who are directly exposed to raw material volatility but have no way to quantify or hedge it.

Step 1

Build Your Bill of Materials

Enter the raw materials in your project — copper wire, steel beams, lumber — with quantities and costs. ClusterHedge treats your project like a micro-portfolio with real commodity exposure.

Step 2

Get Proxy Hedge Recommendations

The Material Correlation Engine (MCE) calculates Pearson correlations between your materials and publicly-traded commodity ETFs, then recommends the most mathematically efficient hedge instrument with a dollar amount.

Step 3

Layer in Global Intelligence

Run geopolitical conflict simulations and watch ClusterHedge map the real financial impact directly onto your active bills of materials — giving you a precise, actionable cost impact before the market does.

The Engine Room

Four Layers of Intelligence

From real-time financial correlations to Monte Carlo war simulations — each layer adds a new dimension of insight to your supply chain.

Layer 01

Material Correlation Engine

Uses live yfinance data and Pearson correlation to identify which ETFs best proxy your raw materials, then calculates the optimal hedge size adjusted by subjective Black-Litterman market views.

yfinance Pearson Corr. Black-Litterman
Layer 02

Monte Carlo Risk Engine

Runs 1,000-pass Monte Carlo simulations on your BOM to generate a 95% Value-at-Risk (VaR) figure. Quantifies "how bad can this get?" with formal statistical confidence intervals.

NumPy Monte Carlo 95% VaR
Layer 03

Spatial Agglomeration Alpha

Uses live GIS data (Overpass API) to calculate the density of similar economic activity within your business's radius — generating an "agglomeration score" that translates to a local pricing premium you can justifiably charge.

Overpass API GIS Pricing Power
Layer 04

Geopolitical Radar

Simulate conflicts between any two nations and watch the engine compute cascading supply chain shocks, alliance activations, and secondary inflationary contagion — directly mapped to your active BOMs within 4 hours of market reaction.
